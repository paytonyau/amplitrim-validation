The twelve trims compared

A trim cuts each read before the first window of bases whose mean quality is below a limit. Twelve were compared on the eight datasets with a known answer: windows of 2 to 20 bases at mean quality 15, and mean quality 12 and 18 at window 4.

Safe: false reads rose by at most 1 point and members found fell by at most 2 points, against the default, on every dataset with a known answer. The best: the highest mean reads kept among the safe trims; trims within 0.5 points are tied, and the one that cuts the fewest bases wins.
